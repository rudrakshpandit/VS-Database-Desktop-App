Glass Dropdowns

Twelve templates in the Glass UI light theme. The top six have no scrollbar at all. The bottom six use an iOS-style liquid-glass scrollbar you can drag, wheel or click.

No scrollbar

Long lists still scroll with the wheel, touch or arrow keys. A soft fade at the edge shows when more items are hidden.

Liquid glass scrollbar

Inspired by the iOS liquid-glass controls: a thin track with a clear, see-through glass capsule knob. The knob grows slightly under the cursor and can be dragged. Each template uses a different track style.
